Analytics choices

Who is responsible

The controller is DoubleTick Solutions Ltd, 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, United Kingdom, WC2H 9JQ. Contact admin@doubletick.org about privacy or your rights.

Aggregate measurement

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate page visits, referring websites, broad country and device categories, and page performance. Measurement starts by default without an initial prompt. It does not use analytics cookies, persistent visitor identifiers, fingerprinting or advertising profiles. Cloudflare removes URL query strings and fragments from analytics addresses; we do not send form contents, account details, searches or custom click events.

Use Analytics choices to turn measurement off in this browser at any time. Existing saved refusals, owner exclusions, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control keep measurement off. A preference is saved locally only when you change it. Clearing website storage removes that preference. Blockers and browser restrictions reduce measured coverage; visits are estimates and do not identify unique people.

Google Analytics and Minilytics have been retired. The site removes their accessible analytics identifier cookies. Turning measurement off stops future measurements after a page reload; it does not erase historical reports. We do not create reader profiles or sell personal data.

Delivery and public crime data

Cloudflare delivers and protects this website and processes routine request and security information, including IP address, user agent, URL and time. HTTP request totals include crawlers and are separate from Web Analytics visits. GitHub hosts source snapshots and scheduled builds. These providers may process information outside the UK under their service and data-processing terms.

The site publishes aggregate crime counts, population estimates and boundaries from the official sources named on the website. It has no reader accounts. Search and filters run in your browser. External source links follow those providers’ privacy notices. If you email us, we use your message and contact details to respond.

Retention and rights

We review aggregate audience exports at least annually and keep them only while needed for a current claim, agreement or dispute. We normally review closed privacy correspondence after 24 months, unless a continuing dispute or legal duty requires longer. We do not export individual reader histories.

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. Contact admin@doubletick.org; do not send credentials or full cookie contents. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
